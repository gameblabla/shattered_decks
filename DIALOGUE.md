# Serena's Guide to the Shattered Decks

*You find a worn notebook near the Sanctum hearth. The handwriting is quick, looping, with a few words underlined twice.*

---

So you picked up a deck, huh. Maybe you found it in a market crate like I did, or maybe it just appeared in your hands one morning. Either way, the cards are talking to you now, and that means you're on the road.

I've walked through sand, stone, fire, and things that don't have names. I've lost, I've won, and I've fused monsters that probably weren't meant to exist. Here's everything I wish someone had told me before that first duel.

---

## The Basics

**Life Points** — both sides start with 8000. When yours hit 0, the duel's over. Simple.

Your **hand** is five cards. You draw one at the start of every one of your turns. If your deck runs out... well, try not to let that happen.

The **field** has five monster zones on your side and five on the opponent's. You can only control five monsters at a time. There's also an equip row, but that's for later.

Your deck needs exactly 40 cards. I keep mine in the Sanctum storage when I'm not using them, and the deck editor lets you swap things around before a duel.

---

## Monster Cards

Monsters have **ATK** and **DEF**. Attack power and defense power. You place them on your side of the field, then you send them at the opponent's monsters.

You can set a monster **face-down in defense position** — that hides its name and stats. The opponent has to guess what they're swinging into. Or you can play it **face-up in attack position**, ready to strike.

Here's the important part: you only get **one monster placement per turn**. That means one monster from your hand onto the field. Equips and support cards don't count toward that limit, so use them freely.

---

## Battle

To attack, move your cursor to one of your monsters on the field, press A, then pick a target on the opponent's side. If their field is empty, you can attack them directly — those hits really sting.

When two monsters battle, compare values:
- If the defender is face-down or in defense position, compare your ATK to their DEF.
- If the defender is face-up and in attack, compare ATK to ATK.

If your ATK is higher, their monster is destroyed and they take damage equal to the difference. If theirs is higher, yours gets destroyed. If it's a tie, **both** monsters are destroyed — I learned that one the hard way.

You can toggle a monster between attack and defense position by pressing TAB when you've got it selected in the tactical top view. TAB doesn't flip a face-down card face-up, though — it just rotates.

You can't attack on the very first turn of the duel. Nobody's quite sure why; it's just a rule.

---

## Support Cards

These are the cards that can turn a duel around. They don't count as your one monster placement, so don't hoard them.

**BRONZE EQUIP** — An equip card. Slaps onto one of your monsters and gives it +500 ATK, +300 DEF. Simple and effective.

**DESERT GUARD** — Another equip. +250 ATK, +800 DEF. Better for making a monster really hard to kill.

**ANCIENT DRAW** — Draw one more card from your deck. Sometimes that extra card is exactly what you need.

**OASIS LIGHT** — Heal 1000 LP. When you're staring at a low life total, this can buy you the turn you need.

**THUNDER** — Destroys every monster on the opponent's field. All of them. It's as good as it sounds.

**MIRROR VEIL** — A trap. You set this, and when an opponent's monster attacks, it triggers automatically — destroys the attacker and cancels the attack. They never see it coming.

To use a support from your hand, select it with DOWN, then press A when it's queued. Equips will make you pick a target monster afterward.

---

## Fusion

This is where things get interesting. Pick two or more monsters from your hand (tap DOWN on each one to queue them), then press A to start the fusion.

You'll get to pick which of your monster zones the result lands on — even an occupied one. If you pick a zone that already has a monster, that monster becomes extra material and gets overwritten.

Some fusions need specific pairs — like a Cockatrice and a Reptile making an Insect Queen. Other times, the elements just align. Two Water monsters with low ATK become a Sea Serpent. Two that are both really strong become the Angel Fishwoman. You'll learn the combinations as you go.

If the fusion doesn't work — if the cards just can't combine — the last monster from your hand lands on the field by itself as a normal placement. It still counts as your one monster action for the turn, so make sure the fusion is worth the risk.

Equip cards you queued alongside fusion materials will stick to the final result. So yes, you can fuse and equip in one chain.

---

## The Deck Editor

Between duels, you'll end up in the Sanctum. From the map, you can enter the deck editor.

You've got two tabs: **DECK** (your active 40-card deck) and **STORAGE** (every card you've collected). Use LEFT/RIGHT to switch tabs. The number tells you how many cards you've got in each.

In the DECK tab, you can remove cards to storage. In STORAGE, you can add cards to your deck. You can have up to 4 copies of any one card in your deck — the game won't let you go over.

When you're in a story duel and you beat an opponent, you might earn a new card as a reward. Those go straight to storage.

Once you're happy, press START to lock in the deck and head back.

---

## Controls

- **Arrow keys** move the cursor around the field or menu.
- **A** confirms, selects, attacks, enters.
- **B** cancels, goes back, exits previews.
- **TAB** toggles a monster's battle position in the top view.
- **START** ends your turn or confirms the deck.

## The Road Ahead

There are five duels waiting for you. Each one is harder than the last.

**Kasem** — Desert road. Your first test. She's tough but fair.

**Anpu** — The Jackal Warden at the plaza gate. She judges by courage, not strength.

**Rahotep** — Stone temple. A war-saint with a formation that doesn't break easily.

**Nadira** — Ember crater. She trades in secrets and reads your moves before you make them.

**Isyra** — The void. The Oracle. She's been watching your path since the beginning.

After Isyra... there's the Demon. I don't know everything about it. But I know the cards remember what the old empire buried, and that seal isn't going to hold forever.

*A few pages later, a newer entry:*

One more thing. Don't let anyone tell you the cards chose you for some grand destiny. I bought my first deck off a crate in a market town. The guy threw in a cracked ceramic cup as a joke.

But the cards do answer. I don't know why. Maybe the old Sun Court baked something into them that doesn't sleep. Maybe they're just old and tired of waiting.

Win your duels, build your deck, and when the abyss starts answering back — make sure you hit first.

Good luck. You'll need it.

— Serena
